BlockBliss Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026

Developer: QM Tech

1. Introduction

QM Tech respects your privacy. This policy explains how the BlockBliss mobile game handles information when you use the app.

2. Information stored by the game

BlockBliss does not require an account or sign-in. The game stores gameplay information on your device, including your best score and number of games played.

3. Advertising services

BlockBliss uses Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial and rewarded advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device information, advertising identifiers, IP address, app interactions and diagnostic information to provide and measure advertising services.

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Advertising consent

BlockBliss uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage advertising consent where required. The choices presented may depend on your location and applicable privacy requirements.

5. How information is used

Information may be used to save gameplay records, provide advertisements, measure advertising performance, and support app security and performance.

6. Sharing of information

Gameplay records saved by the game are stored locally on your device. Advertising-related information may be processed by Google and its advertising partners as described in Google's privacy policy.

7. Data retention and deletion

Gameplay records remain on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. Information processed by Google is subject to Google's applicable retention and deletion policies.

8. Children's privacy

BlockBliss does not require users to provide personal information to create an account. Advertising services may nevertheless process device or advertising-related information. Parents or guardians with questions may contact us.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its services change. Updated versions will be published on this webpage.
